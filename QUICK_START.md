```bash

nano create_hpc_presentation.py

# 2. Regenerate the presentation
python3 create_hpc_presentation.py

# HPC_Presentation.pptx (updated)
```

### Common Customizations
- Add your name/institution (Slide 1)
- Change color scheme (modify `self.colors`)
- Add/remove slides (modify `generate_presentation()`)
- Update content (modify content arrays)
- Adjust diagrams (modify `create_*_diagram()` methods)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### 🌟 Make It Memorable

1. **Start strong** - Grab attention with Slide 1
2. **Tell stories** - Use real examples (hurricane prediction, drug discovery)
3. **Use analogies** - Compare to familiar concepts
4. **Show passion** - Your enthusiasm is infectious
5. **End powerfully** - Leave them inspired

### 🎯 Handle Questions Well

- **Listen carefully** - Understand the question fully
- **Repeat question** - Ensure everyone heard it
- **Answer concisely** - Don't ramble
- **Admit if unsure** - "Great question, I'll research that"
- **Redirect if needed** - "Let's discuss that after"

### ⚠️ Avoid Common Mistakes

- ❌ Reading slides verbatim
- ❌ Turning back to screen
- ❌ Speaking too fast
- ❌ Ignoring time limits
- ❌ Dismissing questions
- ❌ Using too much jargon
